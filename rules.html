SERVER RULES

Ensure you follow these guidelines to keep the SISO SMP Network fair, fun, and lag-free for everyone.

⚖️ [1] Player Respect

Respect all players. Hate speech, harassment, and toxic behavior will result in an immediate mute or ban.

🛡️ [2] Fair Play

No hacking or cheating. X-ray, hacked clients, auto-clickers, and exploiting glitches/dupes are strictly forbidden.

💬 [3] Chat Conduct

No chat spam or advertising. Do not spam characters, abuse caps, or promote other servers/discords.

⚙️ [4] Performance

Prevent server lag. Keep massive redstone clocks toggleable and limit large entity/mob farms.

👑 [5] Staff Authority

Listen to staff. Staff decisions are final. If you wish to appeal a punishment, use the proper Discord channels.

Type /help in-game for commands or /discord to join our community hub!
